## 7. Contributing on behalf of an employer (Corporate CCLA)

If you are contributing **as part of your employment** OR your employer
has rights to intellectual property created in connection with your
work :

- The Individual ICLA above is **not sufficient**.
- Your employer must sign a **Corporate Contributor License Agreement
  (CCLA)** separately.
- The CCLA grants the same rights as the ICLA but is signed by an
  authorized representative of the employer (typically VP Engineering,
  General Counsel, or CTO).

To request a CCLA, email `thomas.signorelli@proton.me` with subject
`[Nokido Corporate CLA]`. We provide a templated document for
signature.

A contributor whose employer has signed the CCLA is automatically
covered for all contributions made within the scope of their
employment — no per-PR signing required.

## 8. Acceptance

The CLA is accepted **automatically** when you post the following
comment, **verbatim**, on the pull request flagged by the CLA Assistant
workflow :

> I have read and agree to the CLA

The CLA Assistant workflow records the GitHub username, timestamp, and
PR number in an audit log (`docs/CLA-signatures.md`, append-only,
public). This record is the legally binding proof of acceptance under
this agreement.
